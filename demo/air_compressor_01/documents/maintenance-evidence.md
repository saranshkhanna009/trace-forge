# Industrial-compressor surge / overheating evidence

## Inspection

Compressor airflow fell while discharge pressure, temperature, and motor current rose. Maintenance found a blocked intake filter. Filter replacement restored airflow and stable discharge temperature during plant-demand validation.

## Repair

The intake filter was blocked with dust and the compressor oil was warm; filter replacement restored airflow and discharge temperature.

## Validation

The corrected machine completed the validation run and the production-quality measurement passed its approved limit.
