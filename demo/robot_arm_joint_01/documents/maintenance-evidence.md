# Robotic-arm joint wear evidence

## Inspection

The robot cell showed increasing Joint 3 torque and positional error. Maintenance found depleted gearbox lubricant and measurable backlash. After lubrication and calibration, the robot completed the validation sequence within the assembly-position limit.

## Repair

Joint 3 gearbox grease was depleted and backlash exceeded the service limit; lubrication and gearbox service restored positional accuracy.

## Validation

The corrected machine completed the validation run and the production-quality measurement passed its approved limit.
