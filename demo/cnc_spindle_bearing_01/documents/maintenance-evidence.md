# CNC-VMC-07 spindle-bearing maintenance evidence

## SB-001

The operator repeated the vibration check with a handheld analyzer. The result agreed with the controller telemetry and localized the abnormal vibration to the spindle housing rather than the fixture.

## SB-002

The spindle-bearing grease was discolored and degraded. Inspection found early race pitting consistent with lubrication breakdown. The tool edge did not show a fracture pattern that would explain the full temperature and vibration escalation.

## SB-003

Maintenance replaced the affected bearing and completed the approved lubrication procedure. Bearing temperature fell during the controlled restart.

## SB-004

The validation cut passed the surface-finish and dimensional limits. Vibration returned to 0.82 mm/s during the validation cycle.

## SB-005

Tool T18 showed normal wear for its runtime and no fracture or chipping pattern associated with the incident. Tool wear remains a routine inspection item, not the primary cause.
