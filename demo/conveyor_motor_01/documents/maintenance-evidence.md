# Conveyor motor overheating evidence

## Inspection

The conveyor drive motor current and temperature rose with increasing vibration while belt speed declined. Maintenance found a seized drive bearing with contaminated lubricant. Bearing replacement restored stable speed during the loaded validation run.

## Repair

Drive bearing was seized and lubricant was contaminated; bearing replacement restored motor temperature and belt speed.

## Validation

The corrected machine completed the validation run and the production-quality measurement passed its approved limit.
