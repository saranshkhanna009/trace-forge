# Hydraulic-press pressure loss evidence

## Inspection

Hydraulic pressure and ram force declined while oil temperature and pump current increased. Maintenance confirmed a leaking cylinder seal and contaminated oil. Seal replacement restored forming pressure during the validation cycle.

## Repair

A hydraulic cylinder seal was leaking and the oil contained contamination; seal replacement restored pressure and forming force.

## Validation

The corrected machine completed the validation run and the production-quality measurement passed its approved limit.
