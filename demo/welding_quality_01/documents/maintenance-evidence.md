# Welding-quality degradation evidence

## Inspection

Shielding-gas flow fell while arc voltage and torch temperature became unstable. Maintenance found debris restricting the gas regulator. The regulator was replaced and the validation weld passed the approved porosity limit.

## Repair

The shielding-gas regulator was restricted by debris; regulator replacement restored nozzle flow and the validation weld passed inspection.

## Validation

The corrected machine completed the validation run and the production-quality measurement passed its approved limit.
