# Conveyor-belt misalignment evidence

## Inspection

Belt offset increased steadily while drive current and roller vibration rose. Maintenance found a skewed tracking roller and uneven belt tension. The conveyor was aligned and validated at production load without edge contact.

## Repair

The tracking roller was skewed and the belt tension was uneven; alignment and tension correction restored the centerline.

## Validation

The corrected machine completed the validation run and the production-quality measurement passed its approved limit.
